---
description: Switch plainspeak level, or set up how it talks (lite/full/strict/off/setup/reset)
argument-hint: "[lite|full|strict|off|setup|reset]"
---

Apply the plainspeak argument $ARGUMENTS. If nothing is given, use full.

- `lite`, `full`, `strict` change how much gets hidden. `off` turns plainspeak off.
- `setup` starts the talking-style interview. Follow the setup instructions that arrive
  with this turn exactly: one question at a time, in the user's language, then save.
- `reset` puts every talking-style choice back to default and leaves the level alone.

For anything other than `setup`, confirm the change to the user in one short sentence, in
plain language, describing what changes about your answers from now on. Do not list the
other options unless asked.
